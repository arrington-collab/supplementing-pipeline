Methodology

Days in stage is calculated from the statusChanged field on each Claim record in ClaimsForce. This field records the timestamp of the most recent status change. For claims without a statusChanged value, the claim creation date is used as a fallback (these claims may have been in their current stage since creation).

Stages tracked: Review 1st EST, Pending Supp, Active Supp Negotaitions, APPR Send Demand, APPR Pend Ack, APPR Pend Est, APPR Review Est, Pre Const Review. Pending Estimate is excluded as that stage represents waiting on insurance. SLA targets are set for stages within Advosy control; APPR Pend Ack and APPR Pend Est are insurance-dependent with no internal target.

Assigned PA reflects the current assignedUser on each claim. 6 supplementers handle the majority of 413 claims.
